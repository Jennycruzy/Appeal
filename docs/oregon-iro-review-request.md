# Oregon IRO case-detail review request (draft)

The Oregon Division of Financial Regulation publishes an official IRO Case
Detail Report and states that redacted case synopsis reports are available to
the public upon request. Use `Exreview.Ins@dcbs.oregon.gov` for the request.
Do not send the downloaded workbook or any case text until the permitted-use
scope is confirmed.

Suggested subject: `Request for redacted IRO synopses and permitted research use`

```text
Hello,

I am conducting internal, non-commercial research on health-insurance denial
appeals and independent-review outcomes.

I downloaded the Oregon Division of Financial Regulation IRO Case Detail Report
from the official page. The report appears to contain case number, review type,
case outcome, case category, and procedure/service/treatment fields. The page
also says that redacted synopsis reports are available to the public upon
request.

Could you please confirm:

1. Whether the current IRO Case Detail Report is the authoritative quarterly
   report for completed Oregon external-review cases.
2. Whether the listed “Type of External Review Requested,” “Case Outcome,”
   “Case Category,” and treatment fields may be used for internal,
   non-commercial model evaluation.
3. Whether redacted case synopses can be requested for a selected set of case
   numbers for that evaluation, and whether they may be retained locally.
4. Whether sanitized derived records, aggregate metrics, or row-level
   evaluation results may be retained or redistributed.
5. Whether Oregon provides an approved redaction/de-identification procedure
   or field restrictions for this use.

I am not attaching the workbook or any case narratives. Please provide a
written response or authoritative documentation that we may cite in our review
record.

Thank you.
```

Record the response date, office/responder, URL or message reference, and a
local SHA-256 hash of the response. Keep the raw workbook and any synopsis
files outside the repository until the reuse decision is explicit.
